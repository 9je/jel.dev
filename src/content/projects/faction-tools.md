Torn City is a text-based MMO with around 100,000 daily players. Factions are its guilds, and they come with a lot of bookkeeping. faction.tools is the management layer for the members, activity and wars that leaders otherwise keep in spreadsheets.

Before it, I ran torn.bet, a large and active third party casino for the game's currency: games, wallets, payouts, and the anti-abuse work that comes with real stakes. It had daily users until new rules on third party gambling meant it had to shut down, and I closed it in line with them.
