Torn City is a text-based MMO with around 100,000 daily players. Factions are its guilds, and running one well is a lot of bookkeeping. faction.tools replaces the spreadsheets.

It has launched, and my own faction runs on it. There is a war room and war reports, a chain monitor, a target board, an armory audit, payouts, a planner for organized crimes, a roster with activity, a calendar and a newsletter.

Before it, I ran torn.bet, a large and active third party casino for the game's currency: games, wallets, payouts, and the anti-abuse work that comes with real stakes. It had daily users until new rules on third party gambling meant it had to shut down, and I closed it in line with them.
